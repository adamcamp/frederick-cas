# Housing Coordinator Guide (DND Staff)

← [Back to Getting Started](../GETTING_STARTED.md)

As a housing coordinator (DND Staff), you're the first reviewer for every proposed match. You approve or decline matches on behalf of your organization before they go to the shelter.

---

## Your Daily Routine

**Every morning:** Check **Matches → My Queue** for matches waiting on your review.

Each item in your queue shows:
- The client's name and priority score
- The program and voucher being offered
- How long the match has been waiting

Click into a match to act on it.

---

## Reviewing a Match

When a new slot opens and the system proposes a match, it lands in your queue first.

Inside a match you can:

- **View** the client's full profile — assessment score, housing history, time homeless, eligibility flags
- **Approve** — sends the match to the shelter for their review
- **Decline** — requires a reason; the system automatically proposes the next eligible client
- **Defer** — holds the match temporarily (use sparingly; the slot stays open)
- **Add notes** — visible to other staff in the workflow

If you decline, the reason is logged and the match moves to the next candidate automatically. You don't need to do anything else.

---

## Opening a New Voucher

When a housing unit becomes available:

1. Go to **Programs → [Program name]**
2. Find or add the voucher for that unit
3. Mark the voucher **Available**

The matching engine will immediately begin ranking eligible clients and propose a match.

---

## Common Situations

**A client was matched but the shelter declined.** The system automatically proposes the next eligible client. Check your queue — a new match may already be waiting for your review.

**A voucher has been open for a long time with no good candidates.** Go to the program and review its eligibility requirements. You may need to expand the eligible population or check whether eligible clients are parked.

**You need to see the full history of a match.** Open the match and scroll to the activity log at the bottom. Every action, note, and status change is recorded with a timestamp.
