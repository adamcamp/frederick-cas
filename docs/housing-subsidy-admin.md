# Housing Subsidy Admin (HSA) Guide

← [Back to Getting Started](../GETTING_STARTED.md)

As a Housing Subsidy Admin, you're the final step in the match process. Once a coordinator and shelter have both approved a match, it comes to you to confirm the placement and record the move-in date.

---

## Your Daily Routine

**Check your queue:** Go to **Matches → My Queue** each day to see matches waiting for your confirmation.

A match reaches you after:
1. Your organization's coordinator approved it
2. The client's shelter accepted it

At this point the client is ready to move into housing — you confirm it happened and log the date.

---

## Recording a Successful Placement

When a client moves in:

1. Open the match from **Matches → My Queue**
2. Click **Record Housing Date**
3. Enter the actual move-in date
4. Save

The client is removed from the active matching pool and recorded in the Housed report. The match is closed.

---

## Recording a Direct Placement

Sometimes a client gets housed outside of a CAS match — through a direct referral or another process.

1. Open the client's profile
2. Click **Mark as Housed**
3. Enter the date they moved in

This keeps your housed numbers accurate without going through the full match workflow.

---

## Managing Housing Units

As an HSA you may also manage the voucher inventory for your programs.

- **Add a voucher:** Programs → [Program] → Add Voucher
- **Mark a voucher available:** Opens it for matching
- **Mark a voucher unavailable:** Removes it from matching temporarily (e.g., unit needs repairs)

---

## Common Situations

**A client fell through at the last minute.** If the placement didn't happen after being confirmed, contact your coordinator. They can reopen the voucher and the system will propose the next candidate.

**You need to correct a housing date.** Open the housed record and edit the date. Contact your admin if you don't see an edit option.

---
