# Shelter Agency Guide

← [Back to Getting Started](../GETTING_STARTED.md)

As shelter staff, you review matches for clients at your shelter. You only see your own clients — not clients from other shelters.

---

## Your Daily Routine

**Check your queue:** Go to **Matches → My Queue** each day to see if any of your clients have an active match waiting for your response.

When the lead organization's coordinator approves a match for one of your clients, it comes to you next. You accept or decline on the client's behalf.

---

## Responding to a Match

Open a match from your queue to see:

- The housing opportunity being offered (program, location, type)
- The client's current status at your shelter
- Any notes left by the coordinator

You can:

- **Accept** — confirms the client is interested and available; the match moves to the final step
- **Decline** — requires a reason; the system logs it and moves to the next eligible client
- **Add notes** — for context that other staff in the workflow should know

Be prompt. Other clients are waiting for the slot, and matches have time limits.

---

## Keeping Client Status Current

Accurate client status helps the system propose good matches and saves everyone time.

If a client's situation changes, update their availability:

1. Open the client's profile
2. Go to **Availability**
3. Click **Park** and select a reason

Use **Park** when a client:
- Has left your shelter
- Was housed through another program
- Isn't currently ready or willing to engage with housing

Parked clients stay in the system but won't be proposed for new matches. When they're ready again, reactivate them from the same screen.

---

## What You Can See

- **Your clients only** — you cannot view clients from other shelters
- **Active matches** for your clients — the full match history and notes
- **Your shelter's housed clients** — past successful placements

You cannot see or change programs, vouchers, or system-wide settings. Contact your admin if you need something you can't access.

---

## Common Situations

**A client was housed directly (not through a CAS match).** Let your system admin know so they can mark the client as housed. This keeps the data accurate.

**A client declined the housing offer.** Decline the match and select "Client declined" as the reason. If they aren't ready to engage at all, also park them in the system.

**You don't see your client in the system.** They may not be imported yet. Contact your admin — they can check the client list or import the client manually.

**You got an email about a match but don't see it in your queue.** Make sure you're logged in with the same email the notification was sent to. Contact your admin if the match is missing.
